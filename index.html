Guided Tour
The globe visits every location in turn, traveling eastward and stopping (with the camera properly centered) at each one — so nothing gets missed, however your locations are spread out.
How fast the globe spins while moving between locations.
Locations within this many degrees of longitude of each other are treated as one stop and shown together (taking turns, see below). Raise it to group more into each stop; lower it to give every location its own stop. Tune this to match how your own locations are spread out — dense regional clusters usually want a smaller value, widely-scattered locations a larger one.
Camera
The camera automatically centers on each stop (latitude and longitude both) as the tour reaches it. These fields only set where the camera starts, before the tour's first stop.
Labels
A location's label fades in whenever it's within this many degrees of wherever the camera currently is — every nearby location shows at once (not one at a time), so make sure this comfortably covers your widest cluster's spread (should generally be a good bit larger than "Cluster Grouping Distance" above).
Also sets how long each stop is dwelled on (more locations sharing a stop means more dwell time). Separately, locations with Priority "Sequential" take turns being shown one at a time for this many milliseconds each — useful for a handful of locations packed too closely together to show all their labels without overlapping.
